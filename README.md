Synopsis
The penguins are tired of always getting smashed by snowballs and eaten by seals. They have decided to finally get their revenge. Help our hero defeat their enemies!

Controls
LEFT MOUSE BUTTON - Shoot your eggs to destroy the snowballs!
MIDDLE MOUSE BUTTON - After finding it, throw the boomerang, a tool that will help you destroy enemies faster.
RIGHT MOUSE BUTTON - Accelerate!

Enemies
Your main enemies are snowballs: they break into pieces when destroyed. 
Beware! After some time, the seal will come. Be careful, it's strong and will follow you!
